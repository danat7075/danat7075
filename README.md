## Featured Projects

### NYC Yellow Taxi Revenue Forecasting

End-to-end Data Science project for forecasting hourly taxi revenue by pickup zone using approximately 60 million NYC Yellow Taxi trip records.

**Stack:** Python, Pandas, LightGBM, XGBoost, CatBoost, Optuna, SHAP, Scikit-learn, Parquet

What the project demonstrates:

- processing tens of millions of trip records;
- memory-efficient data preparation;
- hourly panel construction by pickup zone;
- calendar, lag, rolling, route, and demand feature engineering;
- prevention of temporal target leakage;
- expanding-window time series cross-validation;
- comparison with a seasonal baseline;
- hyperparameter optimization with Optuna;
- model interpretation with Gain Importance and SHAP;
- error analysis by hour, zone, and residual distribution;
- saving production-ready model artifacts.

Final model: **LightGBM Extended Tuned**

Results on the independent test period:

- WAPE: **20.55%**
- R²: **0.955**
- MAE: **116.88 USD**
- WAPE improvement over the seasonal baseline: **33.1%**
